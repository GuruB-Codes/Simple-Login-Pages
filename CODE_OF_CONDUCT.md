# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, friendly, and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Be respectful and kind to others.
* Welcome and support new contributors.
* Give constructive feedback.
* Be open to learning and collaboration.
* Respect different opinions and ideas.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
* Offensive or abusive language.
* Personal attacks or insults.
* Spamming or intentionally disrupting the project.
* Plagiarism or submitting someone else's work as your own.

## Contribution Guidelines

* Keep contributions original.
* Follow the project's folder structure and contribution rules.
* Do not modify or delete another contributor's work without permission.
* Maintain a friendly and collaborative attitude during discussions and code reviews.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to edit, reject, or remove contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Repeated violations may result in removal from the project.

## Contact

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue or contact the project maintainer through GitHub.

Thank you for helping make this project a welcoming place for everyone! ❤️
